Agri Grade

AI-Based Real-Time Crop Image Analytics

Problem Statement
Under PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana), crop insurance assessment faces critical hurdles:

• Subjectivity & Bias: Manual grading leads to inconsistent payouts.
• Time Delays: Physical inspections take 48–72 hours.
• Lack of Transparency: Farmers lack standardized digital proof.
• Massive Scale: Hard to deploy for 140M+ farmers manually.

Our Goal: To architect a real-time, explainable AI platform that automates grading and enforces absolute transparency.

Key Features
  • AI-based crop grading (A/B/C/D) with precise confidence scoring
  • 6-parameter analysis (Color, Size, Surface, Disease, Ripeness, Appeal)
  • Pest & disease detection with automated severity classification
  • Explainable AI (XAI) providing human-readable reasoning for claims
  • Batch processing with statistical consistency checks across regions
